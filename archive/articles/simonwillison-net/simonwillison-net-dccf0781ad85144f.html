I open my email inbox, and I find pitches and articles from ostensibly serious people with ostensibly serious credentials attached to ostensibly serious institutions who want to publish things attached to their reputations and mine, and I see Claude.

— Jack Despain Zhou, Spitting in the Face of Glaude
