Note that the result of the dot product is a scalar; therefore, the quantity is called the closing speed - it expresses the rate at which the relative distance of the two objects is changing. If it’s positive, the objects are drifting farther apart; if it’s negative, the objects are getting closer together. Therefore the term "closing speed" may be slightly confusing; alternatively, this has been called a "signed separation speed", or "normal relative speed" [4].
