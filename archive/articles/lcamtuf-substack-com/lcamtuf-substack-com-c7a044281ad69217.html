Roll a D6.

Roll a d6.

If you rolled “1”:

  • Read the article.

  • Find and quote the weakest sentence. Must be tangential to the main thesis.

  • Say “This makes me not want to read the article.”

If you rolled “2”:

  • Do not read the article.

  • SF Bay Area humble brag: “This reminds me of the time I met Steve Jobs...”

If you rolled “3”:

  • Open with “I have a doctorate in point-set topology. This is utter nonsense.”

  • Fail to explain the concept correctly.

If you rolled “4”:

  • “You no longer need to pay for software. I just spin up an agent swarm.”

  • Your token budget is $7,000 a day.

If you rolled “5”:

“I agree that the emergence of the techno-surveillance state is appalling. But as the lead of facial recognition at Flock, I just don’t know what a single person can do.”

If you rolled “6”:

“I browse with HTML disabled. This site did not load for me.”
