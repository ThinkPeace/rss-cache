"You can't outsource accountability". But it feels like that's what's happening when a disclosure notice refers to "an incident being at a third party", as though somehow that makes it better. It feels like the intent is to deflect responsibility, but ultimately, accountability remains with the party we entrusted our data to in the first place. It's also ultimately them who needs to decide whether to disclose the incident, so in the case of Optimizely this week, even though it's clearly their fault, it's up to the likes of Norwegian Airlines to decide whether they're contacting impacted individuals. And then, in their case, they haven't named the third party anyway! Strange times, I reckon.
