Email is just one of those utilities like power or water where you always expect it to just, well, to just be there. You sign up with a provider and use as much of it as you need, when you need it, and the idea of it being unavailable for any extended period of time is foreign. Microsoft's outage a few days ago was one of those exceptional circumstances, and whilst it absolutely rocked the whole internet, it's far from a reason not to decry using email for most of the things we currently apply it to. But it is a good time to look at where redundancy makes sense; maybe start with redundant channels for alerts about things that could hurt people!
