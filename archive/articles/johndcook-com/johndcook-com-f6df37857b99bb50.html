Suppose you have a graph with n nodes and no pentagons, how many triangles can it contain?

Here a pentagon means a cycle of length 5 and a triangle means a cycle of length 3. This is an abstract property of the graph, not related to the representation of the graph on a plane, unless the graph can be embedded in a plane with no edge crossings.

Roughly speaking, the no-pentagon requirement takes the square root of the maximum number of triangles. The maximum number of triangles in a graph with n nodes is n(n − 1)(n − 2)/6 = O(n³) and the no-pentagon rule cuts this down to O(n3/2).
