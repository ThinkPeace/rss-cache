Upgrade your PHP installs immediately to protect against php2shell

This morning, two critical PHP Security Advisories were published:

  • RCE via OOM corruption (9.2/10)
  • RCE via stale global state (9.2/10)

If you're running PHP for any service, you need to upgrade immediately to 8.2.35, 8.3.36, 8.4.27, or 8.5.12. And if you use Remi's repos on RedHat-derivative Linux distros, you need to switch to official repos temporarily as Remi's on vacation and can't update his community repo. (Reminds me of xkcd 2347).

I'm glad I finished migrating this website from a PHP-based CMS to Hugo earlier this year—having a static website and a minimal commenting system saves on hosting costs and dramatically reduces attack surfaces. Especially in this era of AI tooling making exploit discovery easier.

Within a few hours of people spreading the word about the upcoming security advisories, some people were already showing exploits in the wild, leading to many sites switching off their public-facing services entirely, until they could upgrade.

Between this and the eMudhra certificate compromise, it's been a busy week in the infrastructure world...
