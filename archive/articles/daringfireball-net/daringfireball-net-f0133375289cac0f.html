Sumathi Reddy, writing for The Wall Street Journal (gift link):

The U.S.’s loss of what is known as measles-elimination status is a massive public-health defeat that begs a question: How long will it take to regain it?

Based on other countries’ experience it could be up to five years, say doctors and epidemiologists. But that assumes the federal government is a robust and willing participant proactively working on measles vaccination.

Without that, it could take even longer.
