It’s Pattinson, Robert Pattinson.

The “Batman” and “Odyssey” star has officially been cast as the next James Bond, ending months of rabid speculation about which young actor Amazon MGM and director Denis Villeneuve would hand the keys to the Aston Martin.

“James Bond is one of the most legendary characters in cinema, and to be entrusted with 007 is the honor of a lifetime,” Pattinson said in a statement. “I’m deeply grateful to Denis, Amy, David, Tanya and everyone at Amazon MGM Studios for their belief in me. To say this role is a dream come true is an understatement. I couldn’t be more excited about this partnership and the work ahead with this fantastic team.”

Added Villeneuve, who recently directed Pattinson in the upcoming “Dune: Part Three”: “Before anything else, I want to express my deep gratitude to the remarkable actors who joined us on this journey. We were surrounded by extraordinary talent, but there can only be one 007. I am overjoyed to welcome Robert Pattinson as our new James Bond. He has the timeless soul of 007 and brings a spirit that is completely new. I cannot wait to begin.”

Amy Pascal, who has produced the “Spider-Man” franchise and several 007 films, and David Heyman, behind the “Harry Potter” series, are producing the new Bond film, with Tanya Lapointe executive producing. Steven Knight is writing the screenplay.

Pattinson picks up the 007 mantle after Daniel Craig’s five-movie run as the womanizing super spy, which began in 2006 with “Casino Royale.” Craig’s final turn was in 2021’s “No Time to Die,” which ends with Bond sacrificing himself to save the world from a secret global terrorist organization. “No Time to Die” was also the last film in the series released before longtime producers Michael G. Wilson and Barbara Broccoli sold creative control of the franchise to Amazon MGM for $20 million.

Predictions about who would be the next James Bond had taken over media and the industry in the last month, and a shortlist of British actors took turns as the presumed favorite. Jacob Elordi was pegged for the role in the earliest days of swirling rumors, but reports since surfaced that the “Euphoria” star didn’t even screen test for the part. Jack Lowden seemed like another safe bet. In early September, his “Slow Horses” co-star Gary Oldman claimed Bond was already picked and cheekily added that his “fingers are crossed” for Lowden. However, Lowden’s run at the character was largely overshadowed by Callum Turner, who seemed like a sure pick until news of Pattinson and Nicholas Hoult’s auditions was revealed. At that point, it became anyone’s guess who would come out on top until Pattinson was ultimately chosen. Other actors in the mix included Josh O’Connor, Tom Francis, Aaron Taylor-Johnson, Harris Dickinson and even Harry Styles.

It’s not hard to see from Amazon MGM’s perspective why Pattinson was the obvious choice. He is, almost unquestionably, 2026’s most in-demand movie star and has the box office record to show for it. With help from Zendaya, Pattinson drove A24’s “The Drama,” an intimate yet squirmy original dramedy, to $134 million worldwide from a budget of $28 million. “Primetime,” in which Pattinson starred as “To Catch a Predator” host Chris Hansen, likewise hit big with $61 million globally against a slim $18 million budget. Pattinson also starred as the sniveling Antinous in “The Odyssey,” which did $1.8 billion, and is set to star in “Dune: Part Three” in December, which is sure to pull in the big bucks. (It’s very possible Pattinson earned the part after impressing Villeneuve on Arrakis).

Pattinson also has the big IP experience needed to lead a franchise like James Bond. In 2022, Pattinson debuted as the titular Caped Crusader in Matt Reeves’ “The Batman.” He is set to revisit Gotham for the sequel in 2028. Those doubting Pattinson would land 007 often pointed to his obligations to Bruce Wayne as a reason why he could never take on Bond. However, after several production delays with “The Batman Part II,” it seems Pattinson is ready for a new longterm relationship with another familiar big-screen icon.

“James Bond is one of the most beloved, inspiring, iconic and yet enigmatic characters in literature and movies,” said producers Pascal and Heyman. “The legacy of this character is in great hands with Robert Pattinson. His fearlessness as an actor, his self-deprecating wit and his ability to continually reinvent himself make him the perfect Bond for our times.”
