Paper at heart, flexible by design

Open it like
your paper planner.

Finalist has always been inspired by paper planners, and on iPhone Duo it acts as one. Check what’s next with it closed. Open it up and the whole day spreads across two pages.

No Duo? Finalist works beautifully on the iPhone you already have.

Every pose gets
its own page.

Broadsheet.

Your Agenda and Timeline spread across the inner display. Drag a task across to give it a time. Drag it back to clear it.

Battlestation.

Prop it on your desk. The top half becomes a Desk Clock: what’s on now, how much time is left, and what’s next.

Closed, it’s all still here. The front screen runs the whole app. Check a task, add an event, and slip it back into your pocket.

Your layout comes with you. Each pose remembers its own Daily layout and Planner style. Open, close, repeat.

A little perspective

Your whole day,
on one page.

Calendar, tasks, reminders, habits, notes and weather, all on one page. Open flat in landscape and the Clock and Timeline sit beside your Agenda. Customize sections as you see fit.

Time blocking, in pencil.

Drag tasks onto the Timeline, resize the blocks, and commit when it’s real. Undo is always one tap away.

A page of your own.

Choose the classic Daybook or a newspaper-style Broadsheet. Rearrange sections, fold what you don’t need, and save your layout.

A little help planning.

Suggest Times uses Apple’s on-device models to place untimed tasks in your day. You adjust from there.

A countdown for what you’re doing, or what you’re about to be doing. A glance at what’s next with a checkbox to complete early, or a +15 button to give yourself more time. Desk Clock keeps your day close without asking for all your attention.

Beyond today

Everything else a planner holds.

The satisfying feel of a paper planner, with all the things paper can’t do. Finalist has been around for almost four years, so you can expect unparalleled depth.

Planner: week, month and year

Look ahead, without losing today.

Weekly Timelines lay out days in columns. Weekly Spreads open like a traditional paper planner. Monthly Swimlanes give you planning superpowers, letting you drag, stretch and draw all-day events. Or use Highlights on runs of days in the Yearly Planner for trips, sprints and seasons. Star your favorite layouts and cycle through them with a tap.

Habits that count

Quantity, not just quality.

Track glasses of water, pages read, minutes of exercise or your own unit. Set daily or weekly targets, or limits for things you want less of. Log from Daily, widgets, Apple Watch or Shortcuts. Sleep and Steps fill in from Health, with a bullet-journal ledger and charts to see the bigger picture.

Fall in love with journaling

The best of journaling and calendaring.

Entries, photos, completed tasks and Activity Rings land on each day. Journal Suggestions help you begin. Workouts from Health bring little maps of your walks. When you open your Duo like a book, your journal opens into a two-page spread.

Notes and Markdown

Your Markdown files, in your planner.

Connect a folder or an Obsidian vault. Markdown tasks automatically show up in Daily and the Planner, while the files stay synced. Dated notes appear on the right days. Swimlanes turn files, folders, tags and lists into interactive planning boards, with Pencil support to help you mark out your plans. Native notebooks, Apple Pencil sketches and dictated notes are here too.

Lists, reminders and capture

Catch it while it’s on your mind.

Apple Reminders are right there next to Finalist tasks. Use Smart Lists to see overdue, upcoming and undated tasks of any kind, including Markdown tasks. Share, paste or capture a photo: Apple’s on-device models pull out the useful details. Play Hear Summary to hear your day read aloud, with a transcript.

Pick colors and scenery separately, from winter forests to summer hills. Even choose a perfect handwriting font for your journal.
