What is End-to-End Encryption?

It means your calendar can be read by only you and the people you share it with. The keys never leave your group, so no one else can read your data. Not even OurCal. Read more

Is OurCal’s End-to-End Encryption certified?

Yes. Our encryption is provided by Seald, whose technology is certified by ANSSI, France’s national cybersecurity agency. Read more

Do we each get our own account?

Yes. Everyone gets their own account, and you share calendars by joining the same group. Read more

Anyone you invite. Share your group’s private invite link and they’ll be guided through joining. Groups support 2 to 100 members. Read more

Can I use OurCal with multiple different groups?

Yes. Home, work, school, social: create or join as many groups as you like, each with its own calendars, members, and chat. Read more
