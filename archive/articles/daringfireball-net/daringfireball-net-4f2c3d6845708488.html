Tim Bradshaw and Michael Acton, reporting for the Financial Times:

“Apple set quite a trap for employees,” the complaint said. “Bright young minds thought they would work in Cupertino. Instead they were joining Hotel California: you can check out any time you like, but you can never leave.”

Apple said: “OpenAI’s counterclaims are a transparent attempt to distract from the substance of Apple’s lawsuit, in which OpenAI has already been ordered to stop using Apple’s confidential information.”

So much has happened this year that this lawsuit — which is only in the earliest stages — feels like old news.
