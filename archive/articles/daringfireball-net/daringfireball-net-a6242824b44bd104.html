Hi there. Thanks for checking out our new product, SUNNNY. Our pitch is simple: as a B2B company, you store valuable customer information across a myriad of systems, making it hard to get a simple overview. SUNNNY consolidates your customer data, cleans it, resolves identities, identifies relationships, and presents it neatly for you, your team, and your AI model.

We thought the best way to showcase the product is to let you experience it live. So go ahead and dig in with some simulated data. Currently, we handhold prospective customers who want to try it with their own data. Email us at HeyShun@sunnny.com with details about your customer data systems, and our product management leader Shun Chen will contact you.

We plan a wider release before the end of the year and will share more then. Key point: your data remains strictly yours—we don’t read, train on, or share it, and you can manage your own encryption keys. We’re developing skills and instructions for AI models to connect seamlessly to any system and populate SUNNNY. For now, we have standard connectors for popular CRM systems and a universal connector for any data system.

Enjoy!

Mikkel & Mike
