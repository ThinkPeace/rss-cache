Mississippi prosecutor Scott Colom officially defeated incumbent Sen. Cindy Hyde-Smith (R-MS) Wednesday night. Colom will be the first Democrat to represent Mississippi in the Senate since 1982 and he will also becomes the state’s first Black senator in nearly 150 years.

Colom’s historic win also means Senate Democrats just grew their majority for the next two years. Senate Democrats will now at least have a 55 seat majority in the 120th Congress.

Alaska’s Mary Peltola will further expand Senate Democrats’ majority to 56 seats if she wins. Her race against incumbent Sen. Dan Sullivan (R-AK) has not been called yet, but Peltola is leading with more than 3900 votes.
