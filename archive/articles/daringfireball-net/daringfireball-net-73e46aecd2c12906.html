Emma Roth, The Verge (gift link):

Google is rebranding its Spark AI agent as “Tasks” and making it free to use. The feature, which is powered by Google’s new Gemini 4 Hydrogen AI model, can do things like create meal plans, manage your calendar, and dig through your emails to find recurring expenses you might not need.

Users in the US will get access to a few other features, including the ability for the AI agent to call businesses to compare product pricing and check inventory, purchase items with Google Pay, and sign into accounts that support Google Password Manager. US users can also assign Gemini tasks by emailing gemini@google.com or texting it.

New rule for Google: measure twice and cut once when naming anything. Is there a single product they’ve come out with related to AI that hasn’t gone through at least two entirely different names? And do these ding-dongs realize that “Tasks” sounds like a to-do app — in fact, so much like a to-do app that Google already has one named “Google Tasks”?
