How it works: In the morning - Start on a fresh canvas with the weather forecast, your all-day events, and the interests you want to focus on. - Choose what to work on from your Ideas list. - Begin your day. Your top three items appear on the iPhone Lock Screen. During the day - Block distractions with Focus Shields. - Give each task your full attention in an immersive, full-screen Focus Session. - Keep your current task in view with Live Activities on iPhone and Apple Watch. - Get credit for the time you put in, even when you don't finish a task. In the evening - Close your day. Your Focus Shields are lifted, and unfinished tasks return to your Ideas list. - Celebrate your accomplishments with an immersive 3D experience. - Revisit what you completed, what you worked on, and for how long in your journal timeline. - Tomorrow starts empty. Many ideas – no overwhelm - Organize your ideas by interest. - Your Ideas list stays compact, showing only the top three ideas from each interest. - Dive into each interest for long-term planning.
