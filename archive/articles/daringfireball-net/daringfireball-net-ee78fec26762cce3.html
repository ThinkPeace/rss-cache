Damian Paletta, writing for The Wall Street Journal (gift link):

Trump disclosed in a Monday CNBC interview that he had “protected” Elon Musk in his first term, though he didn’t detail how.

“I protected Elon in my first term, very big. Elon was protected. I talk about it, and I’m proud of Elon. I love Elon. I think he’s great. You know, 80% super genius, 20% he has other problems, but I tell him that. But Elon I protected. You wouldn’t have an Elon if it wasn’t for me, and with that certain incident that took place, which was, you know, a tough incident.”

Trump didn’t go into more detail, but he did say “we have to protect our brilliant people because we don’t have enough.”

As Trump spirals into dementia and he loses whatever modicum of inhibition he started with, we’ll hear more and more nuggets like this. Is there really even a secret Musk scandal here? Probably not — this is probably just Trump being Trump, summoning his inner “John Barron” hype man. But it sure is a crazy thing to just drop on CNBC about one of your own major political allies.
