I'd say in a given week I probably only do about fifteen minutes of real, actual, work. — Office Space

I was watching a video where a guy said he has 8 agents working in the background. He was stressed out and was trying to "get shit done." At one point he said "I have no time to myself."

That was very confusing. Let's assume for a second that the AI agents were doing all the work for him. Maybe it was some tedious work that would have taken him so much time to do manually. Now, he doesn't have to do the work because someone else is doing it. Why is he still busy?

The Future we were promised

It seems like the goal of productivity is to reduce the amount of work needed to create a set of results. But the moment you create the same results in less time, you are incentivised to use the new gained time to produce even more results.

You will rest when you grow up — My Mom.

But now that we are grown up, our employer changed the tune just a bit:

You will rest when you are dead.
