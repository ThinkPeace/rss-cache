In which I introduce an ill-advised, last-minute video series
So. Somehow it's the end of November. Where has the time gone? Apparently having a job, working on Compiler Explorer, and lots of other side projects has made the year go by quickly! In my travels with my employer, HRT, this year I've been to many conferences, meetups, and universities. The thing I'm most asked is: "Will there be an Advent of Compiler Optimisations this year?" So far I've replied that, sorry, no, I can't: I just don't have the time. Each of those videos, the research, the writing, the recording, the editing, and the rest of the YouTube stuff, took like 10 hours per episode1. I don't have that kind of time. But...I do want to do something. So! I am announcing the Advent of Microarchitecture 2026! I'm recording straight to camera with little or no prep, and then using a light touch on editing and fixing in post-production (with a little help from an LLM). All the content is me talking directly to camera, ad-libbing, and then the process of turning that into something uploadable is automated as much as I can. So: don't expect too much, it's very low effort, but hopefully you'll enjoy it anyway! The introduction video is now up (playlist here), and you can subscribe to my channel here2 to ensure you don't miss them when they come out each morning!
